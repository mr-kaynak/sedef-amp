### Added

- Complete rig: Gate, Tide (compressor), Pearl (overdrive), Coral (distortion), Ripple
  (tremolo/chorus/vibrato), three amps (Lagoon, Reef, Abyss), cab simulator with two mics and room
  per cab plus custom IRs, 9-band EQ per amp, Doubler, Drift (tape echo), Fathom (plate/hall).
- Polyphonic transpose (±12 semitones).
- Precision tuner and the intonation workbench.
- Metronome (standalone).
- VST3, AU, CLAP and standalone builds for macOS, Windows and Linux.
- Web UI with preset browser, factory presets and user presets.
