# ADR-0007: Licence is AGPL-3.0-or-later

Status: Accepted

## Context

JUCE 9 is available to open-source projects under AGPLv3 only.

## Decision

License Sedef Amp under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party code keeps its own
licence (see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).

## Consequences

Anyone distributing modified binaries must publish their source. Commercial
forks need a commercial JUCE licence and the agreement of Sedef contributors.
