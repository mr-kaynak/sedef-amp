# Storylines

The voiceover (assets/vo-align.json) was written first; each storyline below carries the same
nine lines. Picked: **A**, because it gives the film one carrier, mother-of-pearl, that starts as
a fretboard inlay and ends as the thing the product helps you fix: the 12th fret.

## A. The inlay (picked)

- **Device / form:** the feature as the hook; object-to-object chain.
- **What the viewer sees:** a rosewood fretboard with mother-of-pearl inlays. The camera dives into
  the 12th-fret inlay, whose nacre becomes a knob cap, then pulls back to reveal the whole Sedef
  rig. The tour moves through pedals, amps, cab mics and time effects, then the music drops out for
  the intonation check, and the film ends back on the fretboard at the 12th fret, now in tune.
- **First two seconds:** a raking light slides across a rosewood fretboard; at "Sedef" the word
  appears as a pearl inlay set into the wood.
- **Beats:** inlay hook → name as inlay → dive into nacre → rig reveal on the drum entry → pedals →
  amps → mics → echo and reverb → formats as type → "intonation" on the music drop → open string,
  12th fret, saddle advice → all strings in tune → mark inlaid in the fretboard, URL.
- **Look:** two worlds: dark rosewood with nacre inlay (no glow; light rakes across the shell), and
  the product's own lacquer-and-nacre UI at 1.6–2x. Display face Bodoni Moda italic, UI face
  Archivo, as in the app.
- **Sound:** warm neo-soul bed (ElevenLabs), Sarah VO, clicks only on cursor clicks, two whooshes
  on world changes; the bed thins out under the intonation section.

## B. The sour octave

- **Device / form:** life before and after; match cuts.
- A guitarist's chord at the 12th fret beats against the open strings (shown as two waveforms
  drifting apart); Sedef measures the octave, the waveforms lock. The rig tour is the middle act.
- **Not picked:** the payoff depends on hearing the beating, which most viewers won't on a phone.

## C. Type on the groove

- **Device / form:** type carries the argument; cut on the beat.
- Module names (Tide, Pearl, Coral, Ripple, Lagoon, Reef, Abyss, Drift, Fathom) land as giant
  italic type on the drum hits, each cut revealing its real UI underneath.
- **Not picked:** strong rhythm, but the product's best shots (mics on the cone, the saddle advice)
  need time on screen that hard cuts on every beat don't give.
