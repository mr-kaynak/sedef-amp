Sedef Amp is installed

  • In your DAW: rescan plugins, then look for Sedef Amp by Sedef. Logic Pro and GarageBand list it under Audio Units › Sedef.
  • Without a DAW: open Sedef Amp from Applications or Launchpad.
  • Sedef MIDI, if you installed it: open it from Applications, or load it on your guitar track. In Logic Pro, load Sedef MIDI (Instrument) on an instrument track and set its side chain to the guitar track.

To uninstall later, run Uninstall Sedef Amp from the disk image. Questions and bug reports: github.com/mr-kaynak/sedef-amp.
