Welcome to Sedef Amp

An open-source guitar amp and effects suite: pedals, three amps, cabs with movable mics, tape echo, reverbs, a precision tuner and an intonation workbench.

On the next steps you can choose what to install. Everything is selected by default:

  • Standalone app in /Applications
  • VST3, Audio Unit and CLAP plugins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins

The plugins are installed for every user of this Mac, so the installer asks for an administrator password. Your presets and settings are kept when you update.
